macOS says "Orbital can't be opened" or "is damaged"

That message appears for apps that aren't signed with a paid Apple certificate yet. To open Orbital anyway:

  1. Drag Orbital into Applications.
  2. Right-click Orbital and choose Open, then Open again. If that doesn't work, run xattr -cr /Applications/Orbital.app in Terminal.
Why does Orbital ask for Accessibility access on Mac?

To notice that you're dragging with Shift held, Orbital watches mouse movement system-wide, and macOS requires your OK for that. Orbital never reads what you type. It also works without the permission: select files in Finder and press ⌘ ⇧ Space.

Where do converted files go?

Right next to the original, with a clear name like photo-1x1.jpg or clip-25MB.mp4. Originals are never overwritten. You can choose a different folder in Settings, from the menu-bar icon.

Click More info, then Run anyway. This shows up for new apps that haven't built a download reputation yet.
